Solkeeper - Mars Outpost Game

Browser 3D game built in two days: keep a Mars outpost alive for five sols, drive a rover to machines NASA left on Mars, scan them and learn what each one teaches. The rovers are 3D meshes generated from NASA photos and scaled to NASA's published dimensions. The ground is real Jezero crater terrain, streamed from a 1.8 GB NASA/USGS elevation map (about 6 MB fetched), and the Earth to Mars signal delay is computed from planet positions, matching NASA JPL Horizons. Every fact on screen names its NASA source. Plain three.js, runs on a school Chromebook. Built with yjay and ghostnet.

Improvement of Image to Transcription Process for Documents

MSc dissertation at Trinity College Dublin, worked on with the Virtual Record Treasury of Ireland. Transcription tools like Transkribus often interleave lines across columns. A geometric layout engine reads PAGE XML, detects columns with one of four strategies, and cuts mean reading-order error 66x (Average Relative Distance 22.49 to 0.34) on two-column pages. Ships with seanc-has, a desktop and browser app for reviewing and exporting corrected transcripts.

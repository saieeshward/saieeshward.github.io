Chika.

A smart comic reader (Chitra Katha) for Android and iPhone. It walks you through a page panel by panel, fully offline. Built with Shreyansh Soni.

Android · beta

Download for Android

Version 0.2.2 beta · about 14 MB · reads CBZ, CBR and RAR5. An on-device YOLO model finds panels and speech balloons, so nothing leaves your phone.

Download APK

How to install
  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (Settings, then Apps, then your browser).
  3. Tap Install, then open Chika and pick a comic file.

It is a beta, so expect rough edges. The APK is served straight from the project's GitHub release.

iOS · sideload

Download for iPhone

Version 0.3.6 · about 6 MB. Apple does not allow one-tap installs outside the App Store, so this is an unsigned build that you sign yourself with a free Apple ID. No paid developer account needed.

Download IPA

How to install
  1. Download the IPA, then install it with AltStore or Sideloadly, entering your Apple ID when asked.
  2. On the iPhone, open Settings, General, VPN & Device Management and trust your Apple ID.
  3. Turn on Developer Mode (Settings, Privacy & Security) and restart the phone.
  4. Open Chika, tap + and import a CBZ or CBR from Files.

Apps signed with a free Apple ID expire after 7 days. Reconnect and reinstall to refresh it.

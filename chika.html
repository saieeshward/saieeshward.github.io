Chika.

A smart comic reader (Chitra Katha) for Android, with an iOS version coming soon to the App Store. It walks you through a page panel by panel, fully offline. Built with Shreyansh Soni.

Android · beta

Download for Android

Version 0.2.2 beta · about 14 MB · reads CBZ, CBR and RAR5. An on-device YOLO model finds panels and speech balloons, so nothing leaves your phone.

Download APK

How to install
  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (Settings, then Apps, then your browser).
  3. Tap Install, then open Chika and pick a comic file.

It is a beta, so expect rough edges. The APK is served straight from the project's GitHub release.
